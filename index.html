Sochnye Momenty Uploader

Uses YouTube API Services

A private desktop tool used by one person, Anatoliy Krotov, to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel Sochnye Momenty.

It is not a public service: it runs only on the owner's computer and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own short videos on a schedule (YouTube Data API, videos.insert).
  • Reads view statistics of the owner's own videos (videos.list, channels.list).
  • Fixes titles or privacy of the owner's own uploads (videos.update).
